# Table for Two

Shared weekly meal plan for Jevon and Jaiden. Week 3 (Mon 9 – Sun 15 Nov 2026): Daryl's macros, prepped meals, a small bank Mon–Thu for Friday and Saturday, and fibre, fullness and water as the week's focus.

- `index.html` is the dashboard (single page, no build step). Open it in a browser.
- `recipes.json` is the recipe database (203 recipes), the same data embedded in `index.html`.
- `img/` holds recipe photos (resized): `pbNN.jpg`, `att/` (swipe-file recipes) and `alf/` (nutrition principles).

## Notes

- This is a snapshot of the hosted claude.ai artifact. Shared ticks and swaps live in that artifact's database, so opened outside claude.ai they save on the device only and the Ask assistant is unavailable.
- 44 recipes show colour tiles instead of photos: 30 had images that came from a source I couldn't find in the vault, and 14 never had one.
