Morning session

  1. ~7amWater and electrolytes. Rice cakes, banana, a little honey and PB2, coffee.
  2. TrainGym 30–60 min later.
  3. AfterEgg-white oats or eggs and sourdough as breakfast. Smoothie if you're short on time.
  4. ~1–2pmMince bowl, stir fry or tuna rice bowl.
  5. ~4pmYoghurt, fruit and nuts, if hungry.

Coach guidelines · what the plan is built on

Our nutrition guidelines

Go whole

Single-ingredient foods first. Protein at every meal, around 1.6–2.2 g per kg bodyweight.

Eat the rainbow

Three colours of fruit or veg per meal. 25–30 g fibre a day, beans most days.

Weekly anchors

Oily fish 1–3×, red meat 1–2×, fermented foods (kimchi, kefir, yoghurt) 3–5×.

Hydrate

Bodyweight × 35 ml of water a day, plus electrolytes on training days. 30–50 g nuts a day.

Parked · test with signal, one at a time

Decisions for later check-ins

Performance is the scoreboard. If training stalls while form and consistency hold, we pull one of these levers.

Integration

One system for food, weight and training

Connect MacroFactor intake and weight trend with training logs so each check-in reads the real numbers.

Timing

Carbs around sessions

Bigger pre- and post-workout carbs, lighter rest days. Try it if strength is flat after 3–4 weeks.

Micros

Micronutrient coverage

Iron, omega-3, magnesium, fibre, colour variety. Audit a typical week in MacroFactor before adding anything.

Rate of loss

Adjust the deficit

If weight is moving faster than ~0.5% a week and lifts are slipping, eat more. If nothing moves in 3 weeks, trim 100–150 kcal.
